The 15-minute weekly tune-up

Same time every week. One person. One sheet.

5 min

Read the flags

Open the review sheet. Sort by "To fix". Ignore the one-offs.

5 min

Change one thing

A rule, a prompt line, a missing field. One change per week is enough.

2 min

Write it down

Date, what changed, why. The change log is the automation's history.

3 min

Check last week's change

Did the flags it targeted stop? If not, roll it back.

Log it in the review sheet. After a month you have a written record of every improvement.
